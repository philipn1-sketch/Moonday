Common questions

How does Moonday predict my period?

From the cycles you log. Until you've logged three, it also uses the usual cycle and period length you set. The more periods you log, the closer the predictions get. Predictions are estimates and are not a form of contraception.

How do I share with my partner?

On your phone, open Settings, then Partner and backup, and tap Start sharing. Send the code. On your partner's phone, install Moonday, open Settings, Partner and backup, type the code and choose My partner's. Sharing is part of Moonday Plus.

I got a new phone. How do I get my log back?

If sharing or backup was on, install Moonday, open Settings, Partner and backup, type your family code and choose My new phone. Without backup, your log lives only on the old phone. Lost the code? Open Partner and backup on the old phone to see it.

Reminders don't arrive.

Check that notifications for Moonday are allowed in your iPhone's Settings, then Notifications, then Moonday. In Moonday, check the reminders are on in Settings.

How do I add the widget or the Watch app?

Touch and hold your home screen or lock screen, tap Edit or Customise, then add Moonday. For Apple Watch, open the Watch app on your iPhone and install Moonday from the list.

How do I restore Moonday Plus?

Open Settings, then Moonday Plus, then Restore. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I cancel the monthly plan?

On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Moonday. Cancel at least 24 hours before the month ends. Period tracking, predictions and reminders stay free.

How do I delete my data?

Everything on your phone goes when you delete the app. To remove the online backup, open Settings, Partner and backup, Delete the online backup. See our privacy policy for more.

Moonday is not medical advice and cannot diagnose. If something about your cycle worries you, talk to your doctor or nurse.
