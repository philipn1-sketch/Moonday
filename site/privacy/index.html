Privacy policy

The short version: what you log stays on your phone. If you turn on backup or partner sharing, it is encrypted on your phone first, so we can't read it.

Last updated 10 November 2026

Who we are

Moonday is made by QuickQuote Group in Australia. You can reach us at admin@quick-quote.com.au.

What Moonday keeps on your phone

Your period days and everything in your daily log (flow, symptoms, mood, sleep, energy, discharge, sex, ovulation and pregnancy tests, weight, temperature and notes), your cycle settings, your goal (tracking, conceiving or pregnancy), your first name if you give one, and your reminder settings. These are stored in the app on your phone. We can't see them.

Moonday has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It does not track you across apps or websites, and it never sells or shares your information with anyone, including advertisers and data brokers.

Backup and partner sharing (optional, Moonday Plus)

If you start sharing, Moonday makes a random 12 character family code on your phone. Your log and cycle settings are encrypted on your phone with a key made from that code, and only the encrypted copy is sent to our database. The code itself is never sent. Without the code, nobody can read the copy, including us and our database provider.

Anyone you give the code to can read your log. A partner's phone receives it to show your phase, your calendar, your next period and your fertile window, and it can't change anything. Keep the code private and give it only to people you trust.

The encrypted copy is stored for us by Supabase and is linked only to a scrambled form of the code, not to your name, email or Apple ID. You can delete it at any time in Moonday: Settings, Partner and backup, Delete the online backup. It is removed for good, for every phone using that code. What's on your phone stays.

Apple Health (optional)

If you turn on Apple Health in Settings, Moonday saves your period days to Health and reads period days that other apps saved there. This happens on your phone, through Apple's Health app. Information from Health is never sent to us, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone.

Reminders, widgets and Apple Watch

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. With discreet reminders on, they don't mention your period. The widget, the Apple Watch app and Siri read a small summary (your cycle day, phase and next period) that stays on your devices. If the Moonday lock is on, the widget hides it.

Purchases

Moonday Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your card or Apple ID details. The app only learns whether Plus is active.

Requests from authorities

Your log is on your phone, and the backup is encrypted with a code we never receive, so we have no readable cycle or health information to hand over.

Age

Moonday is for people aged 13 and over.

Your choices

  • Change or clear any day in the app, or delete the app to remove everything on your phone.
  • Turn Apple Health off in Moonday's Settings, or in the Health app.
  • Stop sharing on a phone, or delete the online backup, from Settings.
  • Ask us to delete the backup for a family code at admin@quick-quote.com.au.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
