## Review notes (for Apple, English)

Moonday needs no sign in. Everything works offline on the phone.

To try Moonday Plus: every new install gets a free week of Plus automatically, so all Plus screens (Insights; Settings, Goal, Trying to conceive or Pregnant; Settings, Partner and backup; the widget) are open during review. The paywall is in Settings, Moonday Plus, with Restore, Terms and Privacy links.

To see predictions, finish the short welcome (pick a recent period start), or log a few period days on the calendar.

Partner and backup is optional. Settings, Partner and backup, Start sharing creates a random code on the device; the log is encrypted with it before upload, and a second device can join with that code. No account is created.

Apple Health is optional, in Settings. Moonday writes and reads Menstrual Flow only, on the device. Health data is never sent to our server or used for advertising.

The Apple Watch app and the widget read a summary the phone writes. The Watch's Log period button and the Siri shortcut "Log my period in Moonday" log today as a period day.

## App Privacy answers

* **Data collection:** Data Not Collected. Everything stays on the device. The optional backup is end to end encrypted with a key made on the device from a code we never receive, so neither we nor our database provider can read it.
* No contact info, identifiers, usage data, diagnostics, location or purchases are collected by Moonday.
